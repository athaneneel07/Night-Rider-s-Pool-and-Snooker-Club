Who Are We

The vision and the engineering team behind the Tattva Grid architecture.

Tattva Grid was founded in a college library by Rohit, Neel, and Aaryesh with a shared goal of building clean, resilient software systems. We believe digital operations should be built on independent logic rather than fragile, pre-made templates.

Today, our frameworks serve as the foundation for modern scheduling platforms, ERP portals, and automated database sync services, allowing teams to scale their operations securely.
