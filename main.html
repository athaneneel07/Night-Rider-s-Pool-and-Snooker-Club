Our Genesis & Architecture

Born in a College Library, Built for Independent Scale

Tattva Grid was founded by Neel, Rohit and Aaryesh with a shared goal of building clean, resilient software systems. We believe digital operations should be built on independent logic rather than fragile, pre-made templates.

Our team replaces complex commercial templates and fragile integrations with clean code, translating administrative tasks into systematic automated workflows to improve organizational focus.
Today, our frameworks serve as the foundation for modern scheduling platforms, ERP portals, and automated database sync services. We manage operational loops for businesses with minimal manual friction, ensuring clear workflows and security compliance across platforms.
