Our Genesis

Tattva Grid was originally conceptualized and architected independently by Neel Athane. Operating as the sole technical architect, Neel independently built the initial software foundations, landing systems, and backend logic.

Recognizing that scaling a world-class enterprise requires multidimensional execution, Neel strategically connected with and onboarded elite specialized minds to finish the ecosystem. He brought on Rohit Badgujar to pilot strategic business development and institutional growth as CEO, and Aaryesh Honrao to pioneer cinematic media production and creative marketing execution as CMO. Today, they operate as a unified founding team, working as founders toward the collective success of Tattva Grid.
