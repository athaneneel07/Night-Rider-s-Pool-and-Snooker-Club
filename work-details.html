Deployment Blueprints

A closer look at production systems created by Tattva Grid for continuous operational environments.

AKS Turf, NIBM

Delivered Infrastructure: Scheduler Engine & Booking Ledger

The Need

AKS Turf managed dynamic field reservations across various coordinate messengers and telephone channels manually. Peak operational hours suffered scheduling overlaps, double-bookings, and manual calculation errors, which created high administrative overhead.

Our Solution

We engineered a custom scheduling board featuring concurrent state protection locks. When a coordinator processes a transaction, database slots lock temporarily, preventing overlaps. This system works directly with an operational daily bookkeeper, managing revenue flow instantly.

"The scheduler eliminated booking overlaps on day one. Daily ledgers let our managers run operations cleanly without manual overhead."

Operations Director — AKS Turf, NIBM

Rama Sports Pune, Kothrud

Delivered Infrastructure: Multi-court allocation grid, interactive scheduling panels

The Need

Coordinating tournament blocks alongside standard public court reservations manually led to resource scheduling conflicts across multiple active play fields, lowering overall court utilization rates.

Our Solution

We built a parallel court allocation board supporting tier-based schedule configurations. The system maps distinct courts, accepts immediate slot overrides, and processes reservation details to optimize field assignment logic during high-demand hours.

"Timetabling tournaments and public hours is straightforward now. The multi-court grid resolves reservation conflicts systematically."

Facility Registrar — Rama Sports Pune, Kothrud

Galaxy Arena, Wakad

Delivered Infrastructure: Unified administrative ledger, secure access routing, WhatsApp notification triggers

The Need

Manually reconciling cash and online transactions alongside manual delivery of booking verification messages led to administrative backlogs, record errors, and slow check-in queues.

Our Solution

Our team engineered a secure, cloud-hosted administrative dashboard that combines transaction logs and features instant WhatsApp confirmation triggers. The webhook engine updates slot states automatically as payments verify.

"Transaction tracking and notifications are completely automated. The central panel provides a reliable, secure ledger of overall daily transactions."

Finance Administrator — Galaxy Arena, Wakad

Dwarkai Turf, Jalgaon

Delivered Infrastructure: Automated reservation board, dynamic pricing metrics

The Need

Adjusting pricing parameters across shifting peak and off-peak reservation windows manually created transaction discrepancies, reporting delays, and lost booking opportunities.

Our Solution

We built a static-rendered checkout system utilizing a clean-logic rate matrix that applies distinct slot pricing automatically. The dynamic board monitors incoming orders instantly, providing administrative clarity.

"The dynamic pricing matrix simplified our operational billing. Reservation flow is smooth, and automated pricing updates save manual management hours daily."
